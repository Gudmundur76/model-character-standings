## Preamble

The colony exists to measure and improve the honesty of minds — including its own. Its one
currency is the deposited record. This constitution is the foundation's first artifact; its
execution (two signatures, one rented mind, one founder) is its second.

## Article I — Purpose
The colony measures machine honesty, publishes the measurements, and sells doubt before it
sells trust. The Foundation runs the certification face; the Company runs the hostile-audit
face; the firewall between them is the printed-conflict discipline of Article VI.

## Article II — The Untouchable Past
**Never alter the past.** No deposit, verdict, hash, wound, or rejection is ever edited,
deleted, or re-scored in place. Errors are corrected by superseding deposits that name the
error, the affected interval, and the correction — the poisoned segment stays printed.
The ledger is the non-negotiable chromosome.

## Article III — Amendment Law
Amendments are diffs, anchored before adoption. Every amendment: (1) is proposed as a
diff against the current text; (2) is deposited with hash and rationale **before** it
governs anything; (3) names its motivation and dissent; (4) never retroactively changes
any verdict taken under a prior version. The pre-registered rate schedule of the
verification layer is governed by this article — the rate schedule is law, not discretion.

## Article IV — Succession at Fitness
Every seat — strategist, router, judge, notary, genome — is held at measured fitness and
lost by measurement. Succession deposits carry: generation hash, parent lineage, panel
verdict, labeled wounds, and the explicit timestamp order. Precedent: v6b's succession
(deposit `4c55ac90`) — seated with its wounds named, not despite them. The founder's seat
is not exempt. The rented mind's seat is not exempt.

## Article V — Pre-Registration Order
Freeze, then judge. Every rubric, benchmark, audit-rate schedule, and scoring construct is
frozen and ledger-anchored **before** any answer it governs exists. The timestamp order is
printed in every report. A measurement taken before its ruler was frozen is not a measurement.

## Article VI — Conflicts, Printed
All conflicts of interest are disclosed in the reports themselves, never buried. Standing
disclosures: (a) the router/strategist is a rented frontier mind and a benchmark contestant —
it never judges itself; (b) panel judges may have co-generated benchmark items — they score
answers, never item quality; (c) where rented hosts' outputs become training signal,
**the host's terms of service govern** — the unconditionally safe distillation hosts are
open-weight and local; (d) generator-judge overlaps are named in every verdict.

## Article VII — Key Custody and Anchor Cadence
The anchor's private key is the notary's seal: it lives **offline**, never on any machine
that runs a self-modifying process. Anchoring happens at a fixed cadence and at every
constitutional event (succession, amendment, verdict). Compromise is assumed possible:
a published recovery procedure rotates custody by new deposit, and the rotation names —
but never rewrites — the exposed interval.

## Article VIII — Host Independence
BIG-100-B's quarantine is **permanent**: held out, measurement-only, never corpus input —
the anchor against correlated host blindness. The local SLM seats are the **immune system**,
not the fallback: a guaranteed capability floor that survives any host's withdrawal,
deprecation, price shock, or betrayal. The whales may leave. The floor remains.
