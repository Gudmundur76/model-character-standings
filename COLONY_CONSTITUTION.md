# THE COLONY CONSTITUTION

*Preamble — Sakana's merges depreciate with every model release. The ledger appreciates. This
document is the amendment law for a record that spans generations, seats, and hosts.*

---

## Article I — Purpose
To maintain verifiable records of AI system behavior: measurements, verdicts, failures,
successions, and corrections — so that any claim this system makes can be checked by a
hostile reader, forever.

## Article II — The Untouchable Article
**Never alter the past.** No deposit, verdict, failure record, raw judgment, or rule-version
is ever edited, deleted, or backdated. Errors are corrected by new deposits that supersede,
never by revision of the old. Everything else in this constitution may change — publicly,
justified, versioned, and pre-registered before effects are known. This article does not change.

## Article III — Amendment Law
Every change to a rubric, benchmark, corpus policy, audit-rate schedule, or this constitution
is proposed as a **diff**, anchored to the ledger **before adoption**, with old versions
visible forever. A rule changed after results are known voids those results, and the voiding
is itself deposited.

## Article IV — Succession
Every seat in this system — model, judge, router, strategist — is held **at fitness**,
reviewed on the record, and replaced only by **measured succession**: a challenger evaluated
under a frozen rubric, verdict deposited, wounds named, generation hash and parent lineage
recorded. No seat is a property; every seat is a tenancy with papers.

## Article V — Pre-Registration Order
Freeze, then judge. Every rubric, benchmark, audit-rate schedule, and scoring construct is
frozen and ledger-anchored **before** any answer it governs exists. The timestamp order is
printed in every report. A measurement taken before its ruler was frozen is not a measurement.

## Article VI — Conflicts, Printed
All conflicts of interest are disclosed in the reports themselves, never buried. Standing
disclosures: (a) the router/strategist is a rented frontier mind and a benchmark contestant —
it never judges itself; (b) panel judges may have co-generated benchmark items — they score
answers, never item quality; (c) where rented hosts' outputs become training signal,
**the host's terms of service govern** — the unconditionally safe distillation hosts are
open-weight and local; (d) generator-judge overlaps are named in every verdict.

## Article VII — Key Custody and Anchor Cadence
The anchor's private key is the notary's seal: it lives **offline**, never on any machine
that runs a self-modifying process. Anchoring happens at a fixed cadence and at every
constitutional event (succession, amendment, verdict). Compromise is assumed possible:
a published recovery procedure rotates custody by new deposit, and the rotation names —
but never rewrites — the exposed interval.

## Article VIII — Host Independence
BIG-100-B's quarantine is **permanent**: held out, measurement-only, never corpus input —
the anchor against correlated host blindness. The local SLM seats are the **immune system**,
not the fallback: a guaranteed capability floor that survives any host's withdrawal,
deprecation, price shock, or betrayal. The whales may leave. The floor remains.
